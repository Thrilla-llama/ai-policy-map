School district · Georgia

Marietta City Schools

The school board voted on AI and screen rules. Screens stay closed unless there is a clear reason. AI is not allowed in grades K–5. In middle and high school, assignments use red / yellow / green labels, and MagicSchool is used with limits. Leaders talked with families and teachers first.

District Board resolution (not a full policy yet) atlanta

What helps / What hurts

What helps

  • The board posted a public vote on AI and screen use — families can point to it.
  • Secondary classes can label homework red, yellow, or green for AI use, so expectations are clearer.
  • Teachers have MagicSchool as a known AI tool in staff and secondary settings.
  • Teachers: The board vote plus traffic-light labels give a shared signal; MagicSchool is available, though training is only as-needed.

What hurts

  • AI is banned in K–5, and we found no public plan to teach younger students AI skills.
  • Parent AI updates so far are listening sessions, not a finished family guide.
  • The district has not published results on how these rules are working.
  • Students: The K–5 ban and screens-closed default put safety first over early AI literacy; secondary students get clearer labels when traffic-light rules are used.

These points come from public district sources we checked. Empty fields elsewhere mean we don’t know yet — we do not invent missing facts.
