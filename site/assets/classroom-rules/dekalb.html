Approved tools: District Copilot and/or Gemini when named on the assignment. Not unless listed: personal ChatGPT / unnamed consumer bots.

Pick one color per assignment

Color Meaning Students may Students must
GREEN — AI OK AI allowed as a helper Use the district tool the teacher names for brainstorm, outline, practice, or feedback Disclose tool + use; final thinking is yours
YELLOW — limited AI only for named steps Use AI only for steps listed on the handout No AI on the graded part unless listed
RED — no AI Unaided work Not use generative AI for any part Ask if unsure — AI here is academic dishonesty

No color marked? Treat as Yellow until the teacher says otherwise. Draft IFBG direction: ethical use, teacher approval/attribution, no AI on assessments.

Always true

  • You own AI errors — invented facts and fake sources are still your mistakes.
  • No faking anyone’s face, voice, or writing; no harassment.
  • Don’t paste private student or family info into AI tools.
  • Disclosure line when used: “I used [tool] for [brainstorm / outline / practice].”
  • School devices / accounts: only tools your teacher names.

Quick examples

GreenGemini for 5 essay angles — you write the essay.
YellowCopilot for outline only — graded draft is Red.
RedQuiz, test, or in-class essay — no AI.
Elementary / unclear grades: No finished public elementary student-AI rule set in sources reviewed — default Yellow or Red per teacher.
Literacy: Gemini AI Certification for MS/HS (launch announced for Sep 21, 2026) does not make every assignment Green — follow the color on the handout.
