Colors (pick one per assignment)

ColorMeaningStudents mayStudents must
Green — AI OKAI allowed as a helperUse approved tool to brainstorm, outline, practice, or get feedbackDisclose tool + use; final thinking is theirs
Yellow — limitedAI only for named stepsUse AI only for steps the teacher listsNo AI on the graded part unless listed
Red — no AIUnaided workNot use generative AI for any partAsk if unsure; AI here is academic dishonesty

If no color is marked, treat as Yellow until the teacher says otherwise. Screens: closed by default — open with purpose.

Always true

  • You are responsible for accuracy — AI errors are still your errors.
  • No faking anyone’s face, voice, or writing; no harassment.
  • Don’t paste private student or family info into AI tools.
  • When AI was used: “I used [tool] for [brainstorm / outline / practice].”
  • School devices / accounts: approved tools only.

Quick examples

  • Green: MagicSchool for 5 essay angles — you write the essay.
  • Yellow: MagicSchool practice questions — in-class quiz is Red.
  • Red: In-class essay or test — no AI.
K–5: Generative AI tools are banned. Student tool use = Red. Still teach real vs fake without opening student AI apps. Home harness tools don’t change classroom Red.
