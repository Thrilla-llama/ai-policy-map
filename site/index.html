Public claims, translated into plain English.

Where we look

We look for public rules in board policies, student handbooks, codes of conduct, and guidance docs. If a deepfake or impersonation ban lives in the code of conduct, it counts the same as if it was in AI policy documentation. If we can’t find a public rule, that doesn’t mean the school is “safe,” and it doesn’t mean they never talked about it.
