What the map reveals

A policy can exist on paper and still leave a student guessing.

01

Clarity is uneven

“Use AI responsibly” is a principle. It is not an answer to whether ChatGPT is allowed on tonight’s homework.

02

Safety is separate

We track deepfakes, impersonation, harassment links, and approved-tool rules independently from academic-integrity guidance.

03

Evidence beats theater

Graduation and attendance outcomes count only when a district connects published evidence to its AI work.
