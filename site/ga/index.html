What strong vs weak policy means for families

When rules are clearer

  • Students: Kids know what AI they may use on homework and tests.
  • Teachers: Staff share the same playbook instead of guessing room by room.
  • Accountability: You can point to a public page and ask fair follow-up questions.

When rules are thin or missing

  • Students: What is allowed can change by classroom.
  • Teachers: Educators may invent norms alone — that is hard and uneven.
  • Accountability: Harder to know who decided what. Many places are still deciding; that does not mean they do not care.
